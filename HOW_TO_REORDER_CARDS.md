# 🎯 How to Reorder Cards

## Quick Guide

### Method 1: Drag-and-Drop (Easiest) 🖱️

1. **Hover** over any card
2. **Click and hold** anywhere on the card
3. **Drag** to your desired position
4. **Release** to drop
5. ✨ Done! Order updates immediately

### Method 2: Up/Down Buttons ⬆️⬇️

1. **Hover** over any card
2. **Click** the up ⬆️ or down ⬇️ arrow button (top-right corner)
3. ✨ Card moves one position

### Method 3: Keyboard (Accessible) ⌨️

1. **Tab** to focus on a card
2. **Press Space** to pick up the card
3. **Arrow keys** to move up/down
4. **Press Space** again to drop
5. ✨ Order updates

## 📱 Works Everywhere

- ✅ **Desktop** - Click and drag
- ✅ **Tablet** - Touch and drag
- ✅ **Mobile** - Touch and drag
- ✅ **Keyboard** - Space + arrows

## 💡 Tips

- **Drag Handle**: Look for the grip icon (⋮⋮) that appears on hover
- **Visual Feedback**: Card will lift and follow your cursor
- **Drop Zone**: You'll see where the card will land
- **Instant Update**: Preview updates immediately, no save button needed
- **Auto-Save**: Changes save to database automatically after 2.5 seconds

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Card won't drag?**
- Make sure you click and hold (not just click)
- Move cursor at least 8 pixels before drag activates
- Try using the up/down buttons instead

**Changes not saving?**
- Wait 2.5 seconds for auto-save
- Check internet connection
- Look for save indicator in the UI

**Preview not updating?**
- It should update immediately
- If not, try refreshing the page
- Check browser console for errors
